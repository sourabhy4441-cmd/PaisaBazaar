This simple calculation assumes your monthly savings remain consistent. It does not include emergencies, interest, inflation or changes in product price.

How we calculate it

Remaining amount = target price − current savings. Estimated months = remaining amount divided by monthly savings, rounded up to the next whole month. Credit category predictions are separate and do not determine this savings timeline.
